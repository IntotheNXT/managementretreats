About Management Offsites

Stronger management teams. Stronger companies.

ManagementOffsites.com is an initiative by Bas Kemme and Marijke Shahsavari. Our aim is simple: to help build stronger companies by helping management teams run high-quality offsites.

That is why we bring together strong content, experienced facilitators and the right match for the challenge. A good offsite should lead to sharper choices, better collaboration and tangible impact afterwards.
